Privacy Policy

Garden Tender — by Cascadia Apps LLC

Effective Date: September 2, 2026  ·  Last Updated: September 2, 2026

Cascadia Apps LLC ("Cascadia Apps," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Garden Tender mobile application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using Garden Tender, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Plant and property data — plants you add to your catalog, property zone assignments, notes, and photos you take or upload for plant/insect identification.
  • Property location/boundary data — if you use the Soil Intelligence or property-mapping features, an address or GPS-derived location and any property boundary you draw on the map.
  • Feedback and support communications — anything you send us directly, including cancellation-survey responses, TestFlight feedback, and support emails.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device and diagnostic information — device model, operating system version, app version, and similar technical data.
  • Approximate or precise location — only if you grant location permission.
  • Purchase and subscription data — if you subscribe to Garden Tender Plus, purchase history and entitlement status are processed via Apple and RevenueCat (see Section 3).
  • Local usage data — your plant catalog, calendar tasks, and cached care information are stored locally on your device, and are not automatically uploaded to us unless a feature specifically requires it.

1.3 Information We Do Not Collect

Garden Tender does not currently require you to create an account. If we introduce user accounts or cloud sync in the future, this Policy will be updated before that feature is enabled.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information above to:

  • Provide core App features (plant catalog, calendar, identification, soil and weather intelligence);
  • Generate AI-powered care instructions and recommendations, tailored to your plant, location, soil, and weather data;
  • Cache AI-generated results (in our Supabase backend) so repeat requests for the same plant/zone don't require a new AI call;
  • Process your subscription purchase and verify your entitlement to paid features;
  • Diagnose bugs, improve the App, and understand why users cancel (via optional cancellation survey responses);
  • Respond to support requests you send us directly.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising.

4. Data Storage and Security

Most of your plant catalog and property data is stored locally on your device. AI-generated care content may additionally be cached on our Supabase backend to reduce redundant API calls. API keys and credentials used by the App are secured server-side/in encrypted environment variables and are never exposed in the App itself. While we take reasonable precautions, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

Locally stored data remains on your device until you delete the App or clear its data. Cached AI-generated content on our backend is retained to serve future users requesting the same plant/zone information and is not tied to an individual account. Cancellation-survey responses are retained to help us understand and improve retention, and are associated with an anonymous subscriber identifier, not your name or contact information.

6. Your Choices

  • Location permission can be granted or revoked at any time in your device's Settings. Some features will not function without it.
  • Photo/camera permission can similarly be managed in Settings; it's required for Plant ID and Insect ID features.
  • Deleting your data — since most data is stored locally, uninstalling the App removes your catalog and local data from your device.
  • Feedback opt-out — submitting TestFlight feedback or cancellation-survey responses is entirely voluntary.
  • Managing your subscription — subscriptions are managed through Apple, not Cascadia Apps directly; see the in-app Settings screen for a direct link to Apple's subscription management.

7. Children's Privacy

Garden Tender is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email below so we can remove it.

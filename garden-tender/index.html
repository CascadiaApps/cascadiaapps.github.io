What it is

A garden app built on real data, not generic reminders.

Garden Tender helps you keep track of every plant on your property and gives you the information you need to keep it thriving — grounded in your actual soil, your actual climate, and your actual weather, not a one-size-fits-all watering schedule.

Build a living catalog of your plants, follow a maintenance calendar that adjusts to the forecast, and pull soil composition and hardiness data straight from USDA surveys for the ground you're actually gardening in.

What's inside

Everything a garden needs, in one place.

Plant Catalog

Track every plant on your property — species, location, and care history — in one organized, searchable catalog.

Maintenance Calendar

Watering, pruning, fertilizing, and pest checks scheduled around what your plants actually need, when they need it.

Weather-Aware Reminders

Live forecasts shift your reminders automatically — skip the watering after a rainstorm, get ahead of a frost warning.

Soil Intelligence

Real USDA soil survey data for your property's exact location, translated into plain-language planting guidance.

AI Plant & Pest ID

Snap a photo to identify plants, pests, and diseases, and get AI-powered care and treatment advice tailored to what's found.

Our philosophy

Gardens are ecosystems, not decorations.

Garden Tender is built around ecological sustainability — favoring native and climate-appropriate plants, minimizing unnecessary chemical use, and treating soil health and water conservation as first-class parts of good garden care. Recommendations lean toward what's genuinely good for your land, your local wildlife, and the watershed it sits in — not just what looks tidy.

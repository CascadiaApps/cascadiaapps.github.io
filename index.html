Who we are

A small studio building apps that take the natural world seriously.

Cascadia Apps is an independent software company based in Tillamook, Oregon, founded by Jed Werner. We build iOS apps that combine rigorous scientific data with thoughtful design — software that treats ecological intelligence as a feature, not an afterthought.

Our work spans gardening, coastal recreation, and location-based discovery. Every app we ship is grounded in real data sources — USDA soil surveys, marine forecasting models, and live environmental APIs — because we believe the best software reflects how the world actually works.
